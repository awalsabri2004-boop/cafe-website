The Story Behind the Camper

It started with an obsession for great coffee and the undeniable magic of crisp morning air by a campfire.

In 2021, we rescued a weathered 1974 camper trailer named "Daisy" from a barn in the Pacific Northwest. Over eight months, we retrofitted her with a commercial dual-boiler La Marzocco espresso machine, custom redwood service bars, and an all-electric solar battery bank.

Today, Nomad & Bean brings cafe-quality pour-overs, botanical cordials, and house-baked pastries straight to wilderness trailheads, alpine weddings, and outdoor festivals.
